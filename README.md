# Hey there! 👋 I'm Rayman223

Welcome to my little corner of GitHub! 🚀

I enjoy building practical tools, exploring home automation, and turning everyday problems into useful software. From energy monitoring and smart-home integrations to self-hosted infrastructure and gaming utilities, this is where I share what I'm working on.

## 🌟 Explore my public projects

### ⚡ Energy & utilities

- **[Energy Cost Management](https://github.com/Rayman223/energy-cost-management)** — Track electricity, gas, and water usage and estimate costs with flexible tariffs, including dynamic electricity pricing. **[Try the live app](https://energy-cost.eu)**.

### 🏠 Home Assistant & smart home

- **[Maintenance Tracker](https://github.com/Rayman223/ha-maintenance-tracker)** — Keep appliance maintenance on schedule using usage cycles, runtime hours, or calendar intervals, with reminders and a dashboard card.
- **[Inventory Manager](https://github.com/Rayman223/ha-inventory-manager)** — Organize your fridge, freezer, and pantry with barcode scanning, expiration tracking, and helpful notifications.
- **[Frient Smoke Detector Automations](https://github.com/Rayman223/HA-automatisation-Frient-smoke-detector)** — Coordinate Zigbee smoke alarms in Home Assistant, including alarm propagation, silencing, and notifications.

### 🛠️ Self-hosting & infrastructure

- **[Unraid Database Offsite](https://github.com/Rayman223/unraid-database-offsite)** — Encrypt and back up MariaDB and SQLite databases offsite, then verify backups through isolated test restores.

### 🎮 Gaming & community tools

- **[Battle-Conquest Mod](https://github.com/Battle-Conquest/battle-conquest-mod)** — A World of Tanks client mod that sends battle results to [Battle-Conquest](https://battle-conquest.eu), helping track tournament and quest progress, including Onslaught battles.
- **[WoT Verify](https://github.com/Rayman223/discord-wot-verify)** — A Discord bot that verifies World of Tanks accounts and synchronizes server roles and nicknames.
